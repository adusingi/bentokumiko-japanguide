About 久美子

Japan, from the inside.

I first shared Japan through food — in 2020 I opened Bento Kumiko in Valence, France, cooking authentic Japanese bento. Life brought us back to Japan, and the catering kitchen closed — but people kept asking me to plan their trips.

I have lived in Paris, Brussels and London, and travelled in many countries across Asia, the Middle East and Africa. I know what it feels like to arrive somewhere new, tired, with luggage — so I plan for that: hotels near the right station, journeys that make sense, honest budgets. We're a travelling family, and that care goes into every trip I plan — for couples, friends, solo travellers and families alike.

What I can do for you お手伝い

One service, done properly.

A custom Japan itinerary — planned by me, delivered as a travel book and an app you follow day by day. I plan, you book directly: you stay in control, and pay the hotel, not a middleman.

01

A plan that fits you

No set tours. Your route is built around your dates, pace, interests and budget — whether that's 5 days in Kyoto or 15 days across Japan.

02

The details, worked out

I research where you should stay and propose the best locations for price and convenience — you book directly. And I plan all your transport, from taxi and bus to train, telling you exactly which pass and which type of ticket to buy, for efficiency and cost. Plus luggage forwarding, so you can travel light, and a realistic day-by-day budget.

03

A travel book — and an app

You receive your trip as a travel book to read and get excited about, and as an app you follow day by day on the ground: routes, times, Google Maps links, costs, and where you sleep that night.

Price: being finalised — enquire and I'll confirm the price for your trip before we start. No surprises.

How it works 流れ

From first message to final trip.

Two rounds of your feedback are built in, so the trip is exactly right before you travel.

  1. 1

    You enquire

    You send the enquiry form, answering a few questions about your trip: dates, who's coming, what you love, your rough budget.

  2. 2

    A call, only if it's needed Optional

    If you'd like to talk your trip through, we'll have a call — and I may suggest one if I need to clarify something. If not, we skip it and I start planning straight from your form answers.

  3. 3

    First draft

    I build your itinerary — route, stays, transport, budget — and send you a first draft. You give your feedback.

  4. 4

    Second draft

    I revise it, and you give your final feedback — the last round of changes.

  5. 5

    Delivery

    The third version is your final trip: your travel book and your app, ready to follow day by day.

Something special 特別な時間

A touch of the beautiful, when it counts.

I love beautiful things — a quiet tea room, a lovely garden, a hotel bar with a view. From time to time, my family and I treat ourselves to a very nice place, and I love helping my guests do the same.

This is not a luxury trip. Most days stay simple and sensible, and your budget stays honest. We just choose one or two moments to make special — and plan the rest around them, so they fit comfortably inside the budget we agree together.

  • An afternoon tea in a beautiful Kyoto hotel
  • One special night in a ryokan or a hotel you'll remember
  • A small, beautiful pause — a tea house, a garden, a view — in the middle of an ordinary day

It's always your choice. If you'd like a moment like this in your trip, tell me — and if not, we simply don't add one.
