How Bento Kumiko began

I first shared Japan through food. In 2020 I opened Bento Kumiko in Valence, France, cooking authentic Japanese bento. Life brought us back to Japan, and the catering kitchen closed — but people kept asking me to plan their trips. That is how this became my work: first for family and friends, now for travellers who want a trip planned the way I plan for the people I love.

I have lived in Paris, Brussels and London, and travelled in several countries across Asia, the Middle East and Africa. I know what it feels like to arrive somewhere new, tired, with luggage, not speaking the language — and I plan for that. Hotels near the right station, journeys that make sense, honest budgets, and restaurants and makers worth travelling for.

A travelling family 旅する家族

Planning for travellers, like one.

Behind Bento Kumiko is a family that travels. This site began as the place we shared our favourite places in Japan, and that is still at its heart: every restaurant, stay and artisan I recommend is somewhere I have been, loved, and would send my own family to. No paid placements, ever.

Travelling as a family teaches you what really matters on a trip: short walks from the station, rooms that work, days that are not overloaded, good food without stress. I bring that care to every trip I plan.

And it is not only for families. Whether you travel as a couple, on your own, with friends or with your family, my goal is the same: to help you have the best possible trip to Japan.

What I do — and what I don't do できること

A planner, not a booking agent.

I plan your whole Japan trip, and I deliver it as a travel book to read and get excited about, plus an app you follow day by day while you travel: routes with Google Maps links, stays, transport, costs, and where you sleep each night.

Stays

I search and propose the best hotel for each place, balancing location, price and convenience. I don't make the booking — you book directly, so you stay in control and pay the hotel, not a middleman.

Transport

I plan all of it, from taxi and bus to train, and I tell you exactly which pass and which type of ticket to buy, for efficiency and cost. You buy the tickets; I make sure you buy the right ones.

Budget

You get a costed day-by-day budget, so there are no surprises — like my sample 15-day trip: ¥233,255 for 2 people — about $1,480 in total for a 15-day trip (roughly $740 each — under $50 a day each).

The details

The things that make a trip work: luggage forwarding where it helps, so you can travel light, and station-to-hotel directions spelled out clearly for every stay.

Not booking

I don't book or pay for your hotels, transport or activities — my proposals are based only on what is best for your trip.

How working with me goes 流れ

Five steps, two rounds of your feedback.

  1. 1

    You enquire

    Through the enquiry form, answering a few questions about your trip.

  2. 2

    A call, only if it's needed Optional

    If you'd like to talk your trip through, we'll have a call — and I may suggest one if I need to clarify something. If not, we skip it and I start planning straight from your form answers.

  3. 3

    First draft

    I build your itinerary and send you a first draft. You give your feedback.

  4. 4

    Second draft

    I revise it, and you give your final feedback.

  5. 5

    Delivery

    The third version is your final trip: your travel book and your app, ready to follow day by day.
